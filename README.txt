luisvicens.com — website files
==============================

What's here
  index.html   the whole site (design, discography, booking form)
  assets/      album covers, your photo, and the link-preview image

Adding a new project later
  1. Put the square cover image in /assets (e.g. assets/new-ep.jpg)
  2. Open index.html, find "EDIT YOUR WORK HERE", copy one project block,
     and change the name, artist, year, roles, Spotify link and tracks.
  (Or just send Claude the details and it will do it.)

Booking form
  Requests are emailed to lav.musicproduction@gmail.com through FormSubmit.co.
  The FIRST time someone submits the form, FormSubmit sends you a one-time
  "Activate form" email — click it once and every request after that
  arrives straight in your inbox.
